Methodology

The technical depth

Full preprocessing, validation strategy, and limitations — for lecturers and data scientists who want to inspect the work.

Target variable

We predict temperature anomaly (station temp − city daily mean), not absolute temperature. This removes daily weather variation and isolates the spatial UHI signal. Training range: −4.40 to +7.78 °C.

Preprocessing

Citizen sensor data filtered for physical plausibility, geographically clipped, daily-aggregated, merged with Sentinel-2 features within ±7 days of each scene. 28.3 M of 30.9 M raw rows retained (91.5%).

Feature engineering (8 features, v2)

Seven spectral and meteorological features plus imperviousness (EU HRL 2018, 10 m):

NDVI
vegetation density (B08, B04)
NDBI
built-up index (B11, B08)
B11
raw SWIR · thermal inertia
HUMIDITY
amplifies UHI on humid days
ALTITUDE
lapse rate
wind_ms
urban ventilation
SOLARRADIATION
heat input
IMPERVIOUSNESS
sealed surface % · new in v2

Validation strategy

80/20 stratified split by year, plus Leave-One-Station-Out CV to measure spatial leakage. LOSOCV RMSE is +18% over standard CV, confirming spatial autocorrelation in the data.

Split
80/20 stratified by year
CV
5-fold + LOSOCV
Metrics
RMSE · MAE · R²

Known limitations

2024 has one Sentinel-2 scene. Cloud cover blocked other passes. 2024 RMSE is measurably worse.
CNN under-fits at 100 epochs. Loss still declining. Transfer learning from BigEarthNet would close the gap.
Grid predictions are flat. Weather features fixed at median across the grid suppresses spatial range.
NDVI direction is confounded. All scenes late July/August. Multi-season scenes would resolve.
Sensor siting errors. Some citizen stations on sunlit walls; cannot be filtered automatically.
Sky View Factor not yet included. SVF from Belgian LiDAR DSM would capture nocturnal street canyon trapping.
